A small adventure in six chapters

Lantern Night

Tonight the whole village lights its lanterns at once, and you promised to bring them. Walk the village, talk to your neighbors, and get the lanterns lit.

  • Arrow keys or W A S D to walk, or click where to go
  • E or Space to talk, use things and go through doors
  • Space or click to read on; number keys pick answers
  • Tap where you want to go
  • Tap a person or thing to walk over and talk or use it, or tap the button in the corner
  • Tap the text to read on

Or jump to a chapter: · · · ·

There are no right or wrong choices. What you do changes the village, and the village can always recover. Six chapters, about an hour, with sound.

A game about skills from DBT (dialectical behavior therapy). It is not therapy and not a crisis resource. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, contact local emergency services (in the US, call or text 988).
